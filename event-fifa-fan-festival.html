About the Festival

The Heartbeat of the
FIFA World Cup™ in Dallas

The FIFA Fan Festival™ serves as the heartbeat of the FIFA World Cup™ within each host city, bringing together fans from around the world in a dynamic and immersive celebration of the game. As the premier fan destination for the tournament, the FIFA Fan Festival™ Dallas offers guests the opportunity to experience live FIFA World Cup™ matches alongside live music, entertainment, cultural programming, food, interactive games, and partner activations.

Located at the Pavilion at Fair Park, the event is free and open to the public. SpaceBoi Fresh & Th3 5taR533d5 are part of the official local artist lineup, bringing DFW energy to the world stage.
